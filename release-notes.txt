<en-US>
Friends — see what everyone you've matched with is up to: out walking, at the park, looking after someone else's dog, at home or away. Your own status sits at the top, so you see exactly what they see. Pick the park you're heading to before you even get there, add a photo if you like, and tell everyone or just a few with one tap.

Birthdays — a card on the day, for you and for your dog, and the whole app fills with confetti and cake.

Photos — pinch to zoom and drag to frame a picture before you add it. Pick several and you can frame each one.

Playdates — Add to calendar now sits beside Open in Maps.

Settings — the background animation now has its own screen under App, instead of hiding in Privacy where its switch could end up off the edge of the screen.

Fixed: playdates no longer crash when you open them, and editing one's location changes it instead of creating a second. Saving a status no longer drops you back into the form, the Invite button does what it says, and a shared place is no longer described as "no place shared". The tab bar sits above the navigation buttons on phones that have them.
