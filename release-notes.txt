<en-US>
Who's outside — let your matches know when you're out with your dog, at the park, or looking after someone else's. Pick the park you're heading to before you even get there, add a photo if you like, and invite everyone or just a few with one tap. Everyone starts at home until they say otherwise.

Birthdays — a card on the day, for you and for your dog, and the whole app fills with confetti and cake.

Frame your photos — pinch, or drag the new zoom slider, to crop a picture before you add it. Picking several now offers framing for each.

Fixed: playdates no longer crash when you open them, and editing one's location changes it instead of creating a second. Setting a status no longer drops you back into the form. The tab bar sits above the navigation buttons on phones that have them, and the calendar reads as an offer rather than an error.
