<en-US>
Friends — see what everyone you've matched with is up to: out walking, at the park, looking after someone else's dog, at home or away. Your own status sits at the top, so you see exactly what they see. Pick the park you're heading to before you even get there, add a photo if you like, and tell everyone or just a few with one tap.

Photos you can reframe — pinch to zoom and drag to choose what shows. Your picture is kept whole, so you can open it again next month and widen the crop back out. Pick several at once and you can frame each one.

Birthdays — a card on the day, for you and for your dog, and the whole app fills with confetti and cake.

Playdates — Add to calendar now sits beside Open in Maps.

Settings — the background animation has its own screen under App, where its switch has room to breathe at any text size.

Fixed: playdates no longer crash when you open them, and editing one's location changes it instead of creating a second. Saving a status no longer drops you back into the form, Invite does what it says, and a shared place is no longer described as "no place shared". The tab bar sits above the navigation buttons on phones that have them.
